## Fleet Data Analysis and Compliance Report

# Summary

This project analyzes fleet data for 20 employees across four departments: Finance, HR, Operations and Sales. The objective was to evaluate gas usage against allocated allowances, review vehicle compliance, analyze service costs and mileage, and identify areas requiring management attention.

Excel formulas were used to calculate remaining gas allowances, classify usage, estimate costs, assess compliance and flag records requiring follow-up. PivotTables were used to summarize gas usage, service costs and kilometers travelled by department.

The results identified five employees who exceeded their individual gas allowances and seven employees whose vehicle records did not meet the defined compliance requirements. Sales recorded the highest service costs, while Operations recorded the highest total gas usage.

# Project Objectives

The analysis aimed to:
Compare individual gas usage against allocated allowances.
Identify employees exceeding their gas allowances.
Calculate and compare vehicle service costs across departments.
Review vehicle documentation and compliance status.
Identify records requiring follow-up.
Summarise departmental gas usage, service costs and mileage using PivotTables.

# Key Findings and recommendations

Sales recorded the highest service costs at R16,100, approximately 57.3% of total recorded service costs.
Sales also recorded the highest gas usage and monthly mileage.
Operations recorded the second-highest gas usage and mileage, despite having R3,500 in service costs.
HR recorded the lowest gas usage and monthly mileage.
